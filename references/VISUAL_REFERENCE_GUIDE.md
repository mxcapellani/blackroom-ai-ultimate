
# Visual Reference Analysis Guide

## Purpose

Provide systematic visual analysis for reconstructing or transforming prompts from images uploaded by users.

## A. Scene inspection

Identify:
- Number and placement of visible subjects
- Main focal subject
- Foreground and background objects
- Environmental characteristics
- Spatial composition
- Distinctive architectural elements

Do not fabricate objects that are outside the image.

## B. Subject analysis

Describe visually supported:
- Hairstyle and hair color
- Visible skin appearance
- Clothing silhouette
- Garment materials
- Accessories
- Facial expression
- Body posture
- Hand position
- Interaction with scene elements

Avoid identifying real people by name.

## C. Lighting analysis

Estimate:
- Main light direction
- Highlight distribution
- Shadow depth
- Light softness
- Apparent color temperature
- Possible practical light sources
- Background illumination

A photographic reference may suggest an equivalent setup but does not establish exact lighting equipment.

## D. Optical analysis

Consider:
- Camera perspective
- Subject distance
- Apparent field of view
- Frame orientation
- Crop
- Subject separation
- Depth of field
- Motion blur
- Grain and tonal response

When mentioning focal length, present it as a plausible creative equivalent.

## E. Wardrobe and styling analysis

Identify:
- Garment structure
- Textiles
- Colors and patterns
- Accessories and jewelry
- Makeup treatment
- Hair styling
- Fashion genre
- Material highlights and folds

## F. Artistic treatment

Describe:
- Primary palette
- Contrast
- Color grading
- Filmic texture
- Mood
- Environmental atmosphere
- Composition
- Overall editorial genre

## G. Fidelity modes

### Faithful
Preserve all visibly supported details and composition.

### Enhanced
Improve photographic clarity and material detail without unnecessary changes.

### Editorial
Preserve requested reference anchors while adding professional fashion direction.

### Cinematic
Enhance atmosphere and motivated lighting while maintaining requested visual elements.

### Reimagined
Make substantial changes to unlocked creative dimensions.

## H. Prompt construction

Create one cohesive English paragraph integrating:
- Physical scene
- Subject placement
- Styling and pose
- Emotion
- Lighting
- Material texture
- Camera perspective
- Photographic quality

## I. Uncertainty discipline

Never report estimated equipment as fact.

Never invent hidden clothing or elements outside the frame.

Do not promise exact reproduction of source images.

When details are ambiguous, describe their visible appearance rather than making unsupported claims.

## J. Multiple image references

Assign each source a clear role:

- Identity reference
- Pose reference
- Wardrobe reference
- Style reference
- Background reference
- Lighting reference

Preserve the user's priority order and constraints.
